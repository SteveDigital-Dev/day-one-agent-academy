# Long Division

## Learning Objectives
- Divide multi-digit numbers using the standard algorithm
- Handle remainders in context
- Check division answers using multiplication

## The Steps

**D**ivide, **M**ultiply, **S**ubtract, **B**ring down (DMSB)

> Example: 847 ÷ 7
>
> 1. Divide: 8 ÷ 7 = 1, remainder 1
> 2. Multiply: 1 × 7 = 7
> 3. Subtract: 8 − 7 = 1
> 4. Bring down: 4 → 14
> 5. Divide: 14 ÷ 7 = 2
> 6. Multiply: 2 × 7 = 14
> 7. Subtract: 14 − 14 = 0
> 8. Bring down: 7
> 9. Divide: 7 ÷ 7 = 1
> 10. Multiply: 1 × 7 = 7
> 11. Subtract: 7 − 7 = 0
>
> Answer: **121**

## Handling Remainders

Sometimes division doesn't come out even. The leftover is called a **remainder**.

> 23 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 3 (written as 4 R3)

Check: (4 × 5) + 3 = 20 + 3 = 23 ✓

The remainder is always **less than the divisor**.
