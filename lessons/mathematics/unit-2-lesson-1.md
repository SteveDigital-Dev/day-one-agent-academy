# Multiplication Facts and Strategies

## Learning Objectives
- Recall multiplication facts through 12 × 12
- Use strategies to figure out unknown facts
- Understand the relationship between multiplication and repeated addition

## What Is Multiplication?

**Multiplication** is repeated addition. Instead of adding the same number many times, we multiply.

> Example: 4 × 3 means 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. We say "four times three equals twelve."

## Key Properties

| Property | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Commutative** | 6 × 4 = 4 × 6 = 24 |
| **Associative** | (2 × 3) × 5 = 2 × (3 × 5) |
| **Identity** | Any number × 1 = that number |
| **Zero** | Any number × 0 = 0 |

## Strategies for Tough Facts

**Doubles:** If you know 6 × 6 = 36, then 6 × 7 = 36 + 6 = 42

**Count by fives:** 5, 10, 15, 20, 25... (ends in 0 or 5)

**Nines trick:** 9 × n = (10 × n) − n. So 9 × 7 = 70 − 7 = 63

**Doubles plus one:** 7 × 8 = 7 × 7 + 7 = 49 + 7 = 56

## Remember

- Memorize facts in order: 1s, 2s, 5s, 10s first
- Use what you know to figure out what you don't
- Practice a little every day — fluency comes with repetition
