# Multi-Digit Multiplication

## Learning Objectives
- Multiply 2-digit numbers by 1-digit numbers
- Multiply 2-digit numbers by 2-digit numbers
- Use the standard algorithm and understand why it works

## Multiplying 2-Digit by 1-Digit

**Step 1:** Multiply the ones place
**Step 2:** Multiply the tens place
**Step 3:** Add the results

> Example: 23 × 4
> - 3 × 4 = 12 (write 2, carry 1)
> - 2 × 4 = 8, plus 1 = 9
> - Answer: **92**

## Multiplying 2-Digit by 2-Digit

> Example: 34 × 12
>
> Step 1: Multiply by the ones (2):
>   34 × 2 = 68
>
> Step 2: Multiply by the tens (10):
>   34 × 10 = 340
>
> Step 3: Add the partial products:
>   68 + 340 = **408**

## Estimating Before You Multiply

Round numbers to check if your answer makes sense.

> Example: 47 × 23
> Round to: 50 × 20 = 1,000
> Your answer should be close to 1,000. (Actual: 1,081)
