# Equivalent Fractions

## Learning Objectives
- Understand what equivalent fractions are
- Find equivalent fractions by multiplying or dividing
- Simplify fractions to lowest terms

## What Are Equivalent Fractions?

**Equivalent fractions** are fractions that represent the same amount even though they look different.

> Example: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6. All three fractions represent the same quantity.

## Finding Equivalent Fractions

To create an equivalent fraction, **multiply** (or divide) both the numerator and denominator by the same number.

```
1/2 × 3/3 = 3/6
2/4 × 2/2 = 4/8
5/3 × 2/2 = 10/6
```

The key rule: **Whatever you do to the top, you must do to the bottom.**

## Simplifying Fractions

To **simplify** a fraction, divide both numerator and denominator by their **greatest common factor (GCF)**.

> Example: Simplify 12/18
> - Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
> - Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
> - GCF = 6
> - 12 ÷ 6 = 2, 18 ÷ 6 = 3
> - Simplified: **2/3**

## Practice

1. Are 4/8 and 1/2 equivalent? Explain why or why not.
2. Find three fractions equivalent to 3/5.
3. Simplify: 16/24
4. Simplify: 45/60

## Remember

- Equivalent fractions have the same value
- You can only multiply or divide — never add or subtract — to find equivalents
- A fraction in simplest form has no common factors other than 1
