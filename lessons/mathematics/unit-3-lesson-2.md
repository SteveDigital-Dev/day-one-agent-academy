# Area and Perimeter

## Learning Objectives
- Define and calculate the perimeter of rectangles and squares
- Define and calculate the area of rectangles and squares
- Distinguish between perimeter (length around) and area (space inside)

## Perimeter

**Perimeter** is the total distance around the outside of a shape. Think of it as the length of fence needed to go around a yard.

**Formula for a rectangle:** P = 2 × (length + width)

> Example: A room is 8 feet long and 5 feet wide.
> P = 2 × (8 + 5) = 2 × 13 = 26 feet

**Formula for a square:** P = 4 × side

> Example: A square with side 6 cm.
> P = 4 × 6 = 24 cm

## Area

**Area** is the amount of space inside a shape. Think of it as how much paint you'd need to cover a wall.

**Formula for a rectangle:** A = length × width

> Example: A room is 8 feet long and 5 feet wide.
> A = 8 × 5 = 40 square feet

**Formula for a square:** A = side × side = side²

> Example: A square with side 6 cm.
> A = 6 × 6 = 36 square cm

## Perimeter vs. Area

| Perimeter | Area |
|-----------|------|
| Measured in linear units (ft, m, cm) | Measured in square units (ft², m², cm²) |
| Distance around the shape | Space inside the shape |
| Use when fencing, framing | Use when tiling, painting |

## Practice

1. A rectangle is 10 cm long and 4 cm wide. Find its perimeter and area.
2. A square has a perimeter of 32 inches. What is its area?
3. Which has a larger perimeter: a 6×8 rectangle or a 7×7 square?

## Remember

- Perimeter = distance around (add all sides)
- Area = space inside (multiply length × width)
- Units are different: perimeter uses regular units, area uses square units
