# Plant Structure and Function

## Learning Objectives
- Identify the main parts of a plant and their functions
- Explain how plants make food through photosynthesis
- Describe how water and nutrients move through a plant

## Parts of a Plant

| Part | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Roots** | Anchor the plant; absorb water and nutrients from soil |
| **Stem** | Supports the plant; transports water up and food down |
| **Leaves** | Make food through photosynthesis |
| **Flowers** | Reproduction — produce seeds |
| **Seeds** | Grow into new plants |
| **Fruit** | Protects seeds; helps them disperse |

## Photosynthesis

Plants are **producers** — they make their own food using sunlight.

> Carbon dioxide (from air) + Water (from soil) + Sunlight = Glucose (food) + Oxygen

Plants take in carbon dioxide through tiny pores in their leaves called **stomata** and release oxygen through the same pores.

## Transport System

**Xylem** carries water and dissolved minerals **up** from the roots to the leaves. This happens through **transpiration** — water evaporates from leaves, creating a pull that draws more water up.

**Phloem** carries food (glucose) **from the leaves** to other parts of the plant — roots, fruits, and growing tips.

## Remember

- Roots absorb water, stems transport it, leaves make food
- Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy (food)
- Xylem goes up (water), phloem goes down (food)
- Plants produce oxygen as a byproduct of photosynthesis
