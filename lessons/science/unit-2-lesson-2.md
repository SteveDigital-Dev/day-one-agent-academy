# Weather and Climate

## Learning Objectives
- Distinguish between weather and climate
- Identify instruments used to measure weather
- Describe factors that influence climate

## Weather vs. Climate

| Weather | Climate |
|---------|---------|
| Short-term conditions (today, this week) | Long-term patterns (30+ years) |
| Changes daily or even hourly | Changes over decades or centuries |
| "It's raining right now" | "This region gets 40 inches of rain per year" |

**Climate** is the average of weather over a long period. One hot day doesn't change the climate; a decade of rising temperatures does.

## Measuring Weather

| Instrument | Measures |
|------------|----------|
| **Thermometer** | Temperature |
| **Barometer** | Air pressure |
| **Anemometer** | Wind speed |
| **Rain gauge** | Precipitation amount |
| **Wind vane** | Wind direction |

## Factors That Influence Climate

**Latitude:** Areas near the equator are warmer; areas near the poles are colder.

**Altitude:** Higher elevations are cooler (temperature drops about 3°F per 1,000 feet).

**Proximity to water:** Oceans moderate temperature — coastal areas have milder winters and cooler summers than inland areas.

**Ocean currents:** Warm currents (like the Gulf Stream) bring warmth to nearby land; cold currents cool it.

**Greenhouse gases:** CO₂ and other gases trap heat in the atmosphere, influencing global temperatures.

## Climate Zones

| Zone | Characteristics |
|------|----------------|
| **Tropical** | Hot and wet year-round |
| **Arid** | Dry, little rainfall |
| **Temperate** | Moderate seasons |
| **Continental** | Hot summers, cold winters |
| **Polar** | Cold year-round |

## Practice

1. Is "it snowed 6 inches today" weather or climate? What about "this area gets 60 inches of snow per year"?
2. Why are coastal cities like San Francisco cooler in summer than cities inland like Sacramento?
3. Name three instruments used to measure weather.
4. How does altitude affect temperature?

## Remember

- Weather = short-term; Climate = long-term average
- Climate is determined by latitude, altitude, water proximity, and more
- Weather instruments: thermometer, barometer, anemometer, rain gauge
- Climate change means long-term shifts in average conditions
